# Production listing — 2.7

Short description:
Download local AI models and connect them to compatible Android apps.

Full description:
Better Intelligence is your local AI model hub for Android.

Download and manage compatible AI models, adjust model settings, and let supported apps use on-device inference. Better Intelligence supplies the brain; chat and research are handled by your connected apps.

MODELS
Discover, download or import compatible GGUF and LiteRT-LM models. See estimated memory needs, download progress and installed status. Pause, resume or delete unfinished downloads. Choose a primary model and a lower-memory fallback.

APP CONNECTIONS
Allow supported apps to request local AI. Control each app's access and view readiness. Reconnect after reinstalling Better Intelligence without reinstalling your other apps; approve access again in BI when prompted.

SETTINGS
Choose light or dark appearance and configure available processing, context and memory options. Use one Wi-Fi-only download switch: turn it off to allow mobile data or Wi-Fi.

Inference runs on your device, with no automatic cloud AI fallback. Internet is used for model discovery and downloads. Models download separately and may need substantial storage and available RAM. Performance and CPU/GPU compatibility depend on your device and selected model. Other apps must explicitly support Better Intelligence's local interface.
